# The Third Crusade (`third_crusade`, start October 1187)

Files: `data/scenarios/third_crusade.json`, `data/events/hist_third_crusade.json` (19 historical events), `data/events/random_third_crusade.json` (4 random events). Built on the 410 modern-geometry provinces; `province_names` carries 174 scenario-specific EN/TR names.

## Setting
Start: Q4 1187, one month after Saladin took Jerusalem (2 Oct 1187), Tyre is about to be besieged (12 Nov 1187 - 1 Jan 1188), Pope Gregory VIII has just issued Audita tremendi (29 Oct). 85 named polities from Iceland to Japan; everything else is native fallback. Default player: Ayyubid Sultanate (Saladin). Playable crusader-side starts: Kingdom of Jerusalem (Conrad of Montferrat at Tyre), Antioch, England, France, the Empire, Sicily, Byzantium, Cyprus, and so on. Turn limit 160 (to 1227); territory victory at 10 % of provinces.

## What is documented and what is a game estimate
Documented (from the secondary literature listed below): the rulers and their regnal dates, birth years used to compute ages (Saladin c. 1137/38, Henry II 1133, Richard 1157, Philip II 1165, Frederick I c. 1122, Henry VI 1165, Leopold V 1157, William II of Sicily 1153, Alfonso VIII 1155, Béla III 1148 ...), dynasties, the ongoing war between Saladin and the remnant of the Kingdom, the vassal status of Mosul (1186) and the Artuqids (1183), the Ghaniyid/Almohad war in Ifriqiya, Danish suzerainty over Pomerania (1185), Aragon's county of Provence, and the Venetian/Pisan/Genoese trade treaties with Egypt.
Game estimates, NOT documented statistics: ability scores (adm/dip/mil), treasuries, regiment counts and compositions, generals' tac/log/siege values, stability, military/civ levels, AI personalities, opinion numbers, claims lists, every objective target. Population, development and buildings are generated by the loader from modern geometry and are not historical figures.

## Province mapping (honest limits)
* `lbn_lebanon` stands for Tyre, Beirut, Sidon and Tripoli together; it is the Kingdom of Jerusalem's only province. The County of Tripoli is therefore not separate.
* `tur_cilicia` stands for Antioch and Armenian Cilicia; Armenian Cilicia (Leo II) is not modelled separately and is folded into the Principality of Antioch.
* `jor_jordan` carries Oultrejordain (Kerak, Montréal) as Ayyubid although those castles fell only in 1188-89.
* `tur_pontus` is used for the Seljuk (Rum) lands around Sivas; Trebizond is not modelled.
* The Danubian principalities (`rou_*`, `mda_moldova`) are given to the Cumans, Wallachia being a Cuman-Vlach marchland.
* `prt_south_portugal` is Portuguese although the Algarve was still Almohad (Silves falls to Sancho I only in 1189).
* `chn_*` regions are modern-geometry stand-ins; only `chn_jilin`, `chn_hunan`, `chn_qinghai` carry Jin, Song, Western Xia. Dali, Tibet, Goryeo's northern border, Champa, Srivijaya, Kediri, the Mongol and Kereit tribes and all of sub-Saharan Africa and the Americas are native states.
* Merged or omitted: Hainaut-Namur is separate but Savoy, Zähringen Switzerland, Sardinia (native), Brandenburg-Anhalt details, Armenian Cilicia, the County of Tripoli, Georgia's Armenian vassals.

## Rulers whose data are weakest (ages and abilities are rough guesses)
Artuqids (Yuluq-Arslan), Shah-Armens (Begtimur), Eldiguzids (Qizil Arslan), Seljuks of Iraq (Tughril III, age from a mid-1160s birth), Khwarazm (Tekish), Qara Khitai (Zhilugu), Bulgaria (Ivan Asen I), Connacht and Ulster, the Indian kings, Zagwe Ethiopia, Champa-less Southeast Asia, the Baltic and Finnic councils (names unrecorded, marked as such), Venice's doge, the Papal ruler's age, and the Kingdom of Jerusalem's ruler (Conrad is used as de facto head; Guy of Lusignan was a captive until July 1188).

## Simplifications of the Crusade's key figures
Richard and Henry of Champagne are not rulers in 1187; Richard is England's heir and Henry II's death (6 July 1189) is an event (`tc_henry_ii_dies`). Tancred of Lecce is Sicily's heir so William II's death (Nov 1189) produces the historical outcome through `tc_sicily_succession`. Saladin's death (4 Mar 1193) and Barbarossa's drowning (10 Jun 1190) are likewise events whose options all, or the historical one, call `kill_ruler`; the heirs are al-Afdal and Henry VI.

## Events
All historical events carry `historical:true`, a dated window, a country whitelist and a source citation. Consequences (gold, prestige, claims, ...) are game effects, not historical records. Random events are `kind` random, have no source and fire only with `mtth`.
